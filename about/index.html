关于「夏日手帖」

名字来自一个很朴素的念头：夏天是最慢也最亮的季节，手帖是可以随手翻的册子。希望这个站点也是这样——不追热点，不赶更新，翻到哪里算哪里。

I’m natsumxd.

I work with code by day, and by night I write down whatever the day left unfinished. This blog started in the autumn of 2026 — mostly technical notes, occasional essays, and a few moments I don’t want to lose.

What’s here

  • Tech — tools I’m learning, traps I’ve fallen into, the click after reading source code
  • Essays — small things in daily life worth a second look
  • Records — reminders and timestamps for myself

About the name

“Natsumi’s notebook” comes from a plain idea: summer is the slowest and brightest season, and a notebook is something you can flip through at random. I hope this site feels like that — no chasing trends, no update quotas, just turning to whatever page.

这是这个博客的第一篇文章。

在一切都流向信息流的年代，重新建一个自己的博客，多少有点逆流而上的意思。但我还是想有一个地方，能让我把一件事说完——不用被字数限制打断，不用迎合推荐算法，也不必担心三天后它就被新的内容冲走。

## 为什么想写

写作是一件很慢的事。你要先把脑子里那团模糊的东西摊开，再一句一句把它理顺。这个过程本身，就是思考的一部分。很多时候我以为自己想清楚了，直到开始写，才发现漏了好几个洞。

所以这里更像一张书桌，而不是一个舞台：放着读过的书、写过的代码、走过的路，还有一些当时没想明白、后来才慢慢懂的瞬间。

## 会写些什么

- **技术**：正在学的工具、踩过的坑、读完源码后的恍然大悟
- **随笔**：生活里那些值得多看一眼的小事
- **记录**：一些不想忘记的时间点

## 关于更新

不承诺频率。写得出来就写，写不出来就去生活。这个博客的设计里藏着一句话：慢慢来，比较快。

如果你偶然路过，欢迎随便翻翻。顶部可以切换中英文，也可以直接搜索。

This is the first post on this blog.

Building a personal blog in an age when everything flows into a feed feels a little like swimming upstream. But I still want a place where I can finish a thought — without being cut off by a character limit, without catering to a recommendation algorithm, and without worrying that it will be washed away in three days.

## Why write at all

Writing is slow work. You have to spread out the fuzzy thing in your head, then straighten it out sentence by sentence. That process is thinking. Many times I believed I had figured something out — until I started writing and found the holes.

So this place is more of a desk than a stage: books I've read, code I've written, roads I've walked, and moments I only understood long after they passed.

## What you'll find here

- **Tech** — tools I'm learning, traps I've fallen into, the click after reading source code
- **Essays** — small things in daily life worth a second look
- **Records** — moments I don't want to forget

## On frequency

No promises. Write when I can; go live life when I can't. There's a line hidden in this blog's design: *slow is smooth, smooth is fast.*

If you wandered in by accident, feel free to browse. The toggle at the top switches between Chinese and English, and there's a search box up there too.
